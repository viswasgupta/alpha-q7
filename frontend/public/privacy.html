Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 August 2026

This page explains the information Alpha Q7 may receive when you use this website and how that information is used.

Information collected

When you submit the masterclass or brochure enquiry forms, the site may collect your name, mobile number, email address when provided, experience level, source of enquiry, and submission time.

How information is used

  • To respond to your enquiry and provide requested course or brochure information.
  • To contact you through the details you submitted, including WhatsApp where you choose to use it.
  • To maintain the website's browser-based administration and enquiry records.

Browser storage

This static version stores website content, administrator credentials, and lead records in browser storage. It is not a server-side CRM. Clearing browser data or using another device can result in missing records.

Third-party services

The website may load Google Fonts and TradingView widgets. Those services can process technical information according to their own policies.

Your choices

You can choose not to submit an enquiry. If you have already submitted details and want them removed from the local browser record, contact the institute using the published contact details.
