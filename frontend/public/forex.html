What is Forex?

Forex (Foreign Exchange) is the global market where currencies are bought and sold. Traders speculate on changes in currency prices through pairs such as EUR/USD and USD/INR. With a daily trading volume exceeding $6 trillion, Forex is the most liquid financial market in the world.

How Does It Work?

Traders analyze currency pairs, identify market direction and execute buy or sell positions based on expected price movement. The training focuses on understanding structure, technical analysis and disciplined risk management. You'll learn to read price action, identify entry and exit points, and manage risk effectively.

Why It's Beneficial

  • Highly liquid global market with 24/5 trading
  • Understand major and minor currency pairs
  • Learn technical analysis for currency markets
  • Develop structured trade setups
  • Learn risk management and position sizing
  • Understand both bullish and bearish markets

Who Can Do It?
